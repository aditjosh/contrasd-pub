                Weekend Update: This Week’s Round-up of Remote Blockchain Learning Resources
 
                
                    Hyperledger
                    |
                    
                        Dec 3, 2021
                    
                

            

            
            
                Welcome to the Weekend Update. Our goal with this weekly post is to share quick updates about online education, networking and collaboration opportunities and resources for the open source enterprise blockchain community.
 
If you have suggestions for resources or events that we should spotlight in a future Weekend Update, let us know here using #HLWeekendUpdate. 
 
Free Workshop: Build Your Identity Solution Using Hyperledger Aries
 
Registration is open for a free four-hour workshop on January 20 about how to build your digital identity solution using Hyperledger Aries. This will be an in depth technical session taught by experts in the community. Space is limited and is filling fast.
 
For more information and to register, go here.
 
Linux Foundation Training Cyber Monday Sale
 
Cyber Monday is a week long at Linux Foundation Training. Get the best pricing of the year on all training and certification offerings. Save up to 65% on Hyperledger Fabric Admin and Developer training and certifications and join others who are developing and managing some of the world’s largest blockchain networks.
 
Details on the Cyber Monday sale, including a listing of all LF Training discounts, can be found here.
 
Hyperledger Job Board
 
Check out the Hyperledger job board, where there are listings for current blockchain openings at Kaleido, Walmart Global Tech, IntellectEU, IBM and ConsenSys, among others.
 
Virtual Meetups 
 
	Saturday, December 4, at 5:00 UTC/10:30 IST: Hyperledger India Chapter hosts “Blockchain Stories 2021 – Blockchain and Sustainable Supply Chains”
	Wednesday, December 8, at 16:00 UTC/17:00 CET: Hyperledger Budapest hosts “Bevezetés a Hyperledger Fabric programozásba és architektúrába” (Hungarian)

 
See the full Virtual Meetup schedule here.
