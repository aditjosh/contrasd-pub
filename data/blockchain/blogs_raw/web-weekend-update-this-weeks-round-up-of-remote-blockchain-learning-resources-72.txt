                Weekend Update: This Week’s Round-up of Remote Blockchain Learning Resources
 
                
                    Hyperledger
                    |
                    
                        Oct 1, 2021
                    
                

            

            
            
                Welcome to the Weekend Update. Our goal with this weekly post is to share quick updates about online education, networking and collaboration opportunities and resources for the open source enterprise blockchain community. 
 
If you have suggestions for resources or events that we should spotlight in a future Weekend Update, let us know here using #HLWeekendUpdate. 
 
Singapore Fintech Festival (SFF) Green Shoots Series
 
The Green Shoots Series, a lead up to the Singapore Fintech Festival (SFF), is a collection of online discussions to share practical insights and authentic stories from leading speakers globally. The Green Shoots Series focuses on key issues and topics affecting financial services, technology and public policy. 
 
The next session, which will be on October 6 at 10:00 AM Singapore Standard Time, is part of a series on Digital Currency and will focus on “Standards and Interoperability in the Journey to Ubiquitous Adoption.” The discussion will be moderated by Andrew McCormack of the BIS Innovation Hub in Singapore will include the Linux Foundation’s Brian Behlendorf and Sheila Warren, Head of Data, Blockchain and Digital Assets for the World Economic Forum. 
 
Go here for more details.
 
New Hyperledger FireFly project 
 
The newest project, Hyperledger FireFly, became official this week. FireFly is a multi-party system for enterprise data flows, meaning it provides a purpose-built system upon which to build decentralized blockchain applications. Contributed by Kaleido to Hyperledger Labs in June 2021, FireFly has an active and growing open source community and is looking for more contributors to get involved in the project.
 
Find out more here.
 
Video introduction to using Hyperledger Cactus 
 
Check out this quick video to learn how to create production-ready DLT applications that interact with multiple ledgers with Hyperledger Cactus.
 
Virtual Meetups 
 
	Thursday, October 7, at 9:00 UTC / 18:00 JST: Hyperledger Tokyo hosts “10月7日オンライン開催 Hyperledger Tokyo Meetup” (Japanese)
	Thursday, October 7, at 22:00 UTC / 19:00 BRT: Hyperledger Brazil Chapter hosts “Projeto Diário de Bordo Digital da Agência Nacional de Aviação Civil” (Portuguese)

 
See the full Virtual Meetup schedule here.
