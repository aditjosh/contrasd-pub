                Weekend Update: This Week’s Round-up of Remote Blockchain Learning Resources
 
                
                    Hyperledger
                    |
                    
                        Oct 29, 2021
                    
                

            

            
            
                Welcome to the Weekend Update. Our goal with this weekly post is to share quick updates about online education, networking and collaboration opportunities and resources for the open source enterprise blockchain community. 
 
If you have suggestions for resources or events that we should spotlight in a future Weekend Update, let us know here using #HLWeekendUpdate. 
 
Hyperledger In-depth: An hour with ConsenSys – Part two of a series on Hyperledger & Ethereum
 
ConsenSys is hosting a three-part webinar series focused on educating attendees on various use cases of Ethereum Mainnet and Hyperledger Besu. The series, “Hyperledger & Ethereum: Who Put Peanut Butter in my Jelly?” will go into detail on public mainnet deployments, including interoperability and security challenges to keep in mind. It will also cover DeFi and NFTs as well as recent technical developments.
 
Tune in for the second session, “Deep Dive into the Unprecedented DeFi Growth,” on Wednesday, November 3, at 8:00 AM Pacific Time. For more information and to register, go here.
 
Hong Kong FinTech Week
 
In its sixth edition, Hong Kong FinTech Week will completely reinvent your previous event experience by combining a physical conference in Hong Kong with access online from anywhere in the world. The event, being held from November 3-5, will have a mix of content for in-person and virtual audiences. Sessions available to both audiences include a November 4 panel moderated by Emmanuelle Pecenicic, Head of Digital Strategy / Co-chair Blockchain Committee, BNP Paribas Asset with Hyperledger’s Daniela Barbosa and speakers from ConsenSys, R3 and HashKey will serve as the panelists.
 
For more information, go here. 
 
Wharton Executive Education “Economics of Blockchain and Digital Assets” online certificate program (with discount code)
 
The Wharton Executive Education program at the University of Pennsylvania just launched the Economics of Blockchain and Digital Assets online certificate program.  The six week program will include 50+ lecture videos, seven case studies and crypto valuation models. 
 
Learn more here. Save 10% off with the code: HYPERLEDGERCOMMUNITY
 

Case Study: How Tech Mahindra Deployed Hyperledger Fabric for the Digital Transformation of Abu Dhabi’s Land Registry
 
Learn more about Tech Mahindra’s work creating a Hyperledger Fabric-based land registry for Abu Dhabi and how this fits into wider efforts to digitize government services in this new case study.
 
Virtual Meetups 
 
	Thursday, November 4, at 16:00 UTC / 17:00 CET: Hyperledger Budapest hosts “Deploying Hyperledger Fabric on Kubernetes 2.3+ with a Kubernetes Operator”
	Thursday, November 4, at 22:30 UTC / 18:30 EDT: Hyperledger Boston hosts “What are Blockchain Employers Looking for in a Candidate?”

 
See the full Virtual Meetup schedule here.
