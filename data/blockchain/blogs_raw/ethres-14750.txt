              Hi everyone,

We have developed a tool for web3 developers for easy interaction with any smart contracts in evm. We support 30+ evm chains( mainnet and testnets)

https://web3client.app

You can,

	Create ABIs with methods
	Execute them using metamask connection
	Switch between chains and contracts easily
	Share your ABIs to anyone to try it.
	Execute the contract methods on the in your mobile too.
	You can also import ABIs from standard(ERC20, ERC 721, Erc 1155) or from Chains directly if your contract is verified.


A lot in the roadmap.

Do checkout and share your early feedbacks.

Discord : web3client
