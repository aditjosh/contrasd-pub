          Just Introduced: Refreshed Hyperledger Project Logos

        

        
           Written by Daniela Barbosa, Executive Director at Hyperledger Foundation and General Manager, Blockchain and Identity for the Linux Foundation | Apr 23, 2024 2:18:48 PM 
        

        
          Last year, we introduced a new look for Hyperledger Foundation that fit with our increasingly mature market and ecosystem as well as our enterprise audience. Now, with the input of our developer communities, we have refreshed and, in some cases, reinvented the logos for our 13 projects. 


These updated logos bring a unified look to the whole Hyperledger ecosystem. For some projects, namely Hyperledger AnonCreds, Hyperledger Aries, Hyperledger Fabric and Hyperledger Indy, the community has opted to redesign their logos. The new looks reflect the evolution of these projects and the market. They embody core brand attributes such as performance, scalability and trust.

Look for these new logos on the Hyperledger Foundation website but also the wiki, Github, documentation and the Hyperledger Store. To download them for your own content and channels, please go here.
