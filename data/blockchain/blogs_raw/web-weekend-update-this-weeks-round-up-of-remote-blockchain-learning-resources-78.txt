                Weekend Update: This Week’s Round-up of Remote Blockchain Learning Resources
 
                
                    Hyperledger
                    |
                    
                        Nov 12, 2021
                    
                

            

            
            
                Welcome to the Weekend Update. Our goal with this weekly post is to share quick updates about online education, networking and collaboration opportunities and resources for the open source enterprise blockchain community. 
 
If you have suggestions for resources or events that we should spotlight in a future Weekend Update, let us know here using #HLWeekendUpdate. 
 
Hyperledger In-depth: An hour with ConsenSys – Part three of a series on Hyperledger & Ethereum
 
ConsenSys is hosting a three-part webinar series focused on educating attendees on various use cases of Ethereum Mainnet and Hyperledger Besu. The series, “Hyperledger & Ethereum: Who Put Peanut Butter in my Jelly?” will go into detail on public mainnet deployments, including interoperability and security challenges to keep in mind. It will also cover DeFi and NFTs as well as recent technical developments.
 
Tune in for the third session, “NFTs: A new milestone to be achieved for blockchain adoption?” on Tuesday, November 16, at 8:00 AM PST. For more information and to register, go here.
 
Fireside Chat: Wharton Executive Education “the Economics of Blockchain and Digital Assets” program with Dr. Cathy Barrera
 
In a preview of the Wharton Executive Education “The Economics of Blockchain and Digital Assets” course, the program director, Dr. Cathy Barrera, founding economist at Prysm Group, will join Hyperledger Foundation Executive Director Daniela Barbosa for a fireside chat. Speakers will also include James Edwards from Soramitsu and Emiliano Vernini from Poste Italiane who will discuss their deployments of Hyperledger technologies, which will be covered as case studies in the course.
 
Go here for more details on the session, which will take place on Wednesday, November 17, at 11:00 AM EST.
 
Case Study: Splunk correlates data across all datasets—including Hyperledger Fabric
 
Learn more about how Splunk has added Hyperledger Fabric data into the enterprise visibility mix and how that helped S&P Global introduce a new, secure blockchain-based content management system in this new case study.
 
Virtual Meetups 
 
	Saturday, November 13, at 5:00 UTC / 10:30 IST: Hyperledger India Chapter hosts “Blockchain Stories 2021 & Hyperledger India Chapter 3rd Anniversary”
	Thursday, November 18, at 23:30 UTC / 18:30 EST: Hyperledger Princeton hosts “Blockchain Employment Series: How to Market Yourself for Opportunities”

 
See the full Virtual Meetup schedule here.
